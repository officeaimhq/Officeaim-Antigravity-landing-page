Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash makes clips up to 15 seconds

AI Video Published on November 26, 2026 • Reel Studio Explainer
Video coming soon Watch our channel on YouTube →
Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash makes clips up to 15 seconds and Google says it costs 30% less, but there is no free tier.
Source Note: Performance and savings statements are Google-reported and were not independently benchmarked. Checked against Google's blog and documentation on November 26, 2026.

Access and cost

The model is generally available to developers on the paid tier of the Gemini API, with no free-tier access for this model. Video output at 720p costs about $0.07 per second, which Google reports is 30% lower than Omni 1.1 Flash.

Features

Single generations can produce video up to 15 seconds (10 seconds on Omni 1.1 Flash) with synchronized native audio. The model supports multi-turn conversational editing, up to six reference images, and text, image and video inputs.

Where the limits are

A single generation can be set from 3 to 15 seconds, while first and last frame interpolation and each extension turn are limited to 3 to 10 seconds.

Verdict

The best tool depends on clip length, editing workflow, reference needs and budget; test reference consistency on representative footage before scaling.

3 Key Takeaways

  • Prototype first: draft at 360p or 720p.
  • Cost it out: count duration and likely revisions.
  • Test consistency: use representative footage before you scale.

Sources: Google AI Blog · Gemini API pricing · Gemini Omni guide
